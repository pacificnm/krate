# Klang: roadmap

Each milestone ends with something you can run and check, and is committed and pushed when done. The other instruments' tests and the rack's stay green throughout.

| # | Milestone | Done when |
| --- | --- | --- |
| L1 ✅ | **The pads** | `klang_dsp`: the parameter table (36) with formats; samples handed over and freed safely; 16 pad voices with the three modes, SYNC, REVERSE, START / END, envelope, choke; the filter, drive, bitcrusher, ring modulator; the trance gate; delay and reverb sends; notes 36–51 and Chromatic keys. `klang-render` (generated test sounds) and `klang_tst_pads` (pitch and sync, reverse, loop seams without clicks, choke, crush, gate timing, no allocation on swap). Result: as planned; each pad has two voices (a new hit fades the last out over 3 ms while it starts); `klang-render` plays a kick, a clank, a noise burst and a gated drone it makes itself (peak −3.2 dBFS); `--bench` 4.2 % of a core with all 16 pads looping through every effect; `klang_tst_pads` (23 tests) |
| L2 ✅ | **Patterns** | 16 patterns of up to 64 steps: accent, repeat, reverse, pitch per step, swing, the gate pattern per pattern; pattern change at the end of the pass; Program Change; `klang_tst_patterns`. Result: as planned; the steps are timed by the engine itself (each absolute step's beat, the off-beat sixteenths moved by swing) rather than `StepClock`, so swing, repeats and the Gate pads' releases all land on their sample; joining a running transport starts on the next bar; the rack's RUN for Klang switches it off; `klang_tst_patterns` (11 tests) |
| L3 ✅ | **Samples, panel, the rack** | The sample library (WAV and MP3, file dialog and drag and drop), `pads.json` and `patterns.json`, slicing (equal and at the hits); the `Klang` QML module: pads, the edited pad's controls, the step grid with its pages and step editing, the gate pattern; the `klang` standalone app; Klang in the Krate rack on channel 11. Result: as planned; the pads' controllers are `KlangPadController` ("Pads") and `KlangPatternController` ("Patterns"); a file dropped on a pad loads there, anywhere else onto the edited pad, several onto the pads after it; a sample is decoded once and shared by its slices; slicing at the hits finds jumps in 5 ms energy windows (at least 60 ms apart); a click on a step does what the tool says (STEP, ACCENT, ROLL, REVERSE, PITCH + / −); Klang sits between Kobalt and Krunch in the rack; `klang_tst_klang` (7 tests: loading and the library, pads.json, equal and hit slices, patterns.json, the tools). MP3 loading goes through the song player's decoder; `loadsAnMp3` checks it with a file of your own (`KLANG_TEST_MP3=…`, skipped otherwise): checked with a 2-minute song, cut at 120 s as designed, and played |
| L4 | **Starter kit, manual, docs** | A starter kit Klang generates on first run (industrial hits, a break, a drone, a voice-like texture: no third-party samples); `docs/MANUAL.md` (HELP); README and the suite docs; `krate-render --klang` |
