# Konnekt: roadmap

Each milestone ends with something you can run and check, and is committed and pushed when done.

| # | Milestone | Done when |
| --- | --- | --- |
| N1 ✅ | **The engine** | `konnekt_dsp`: modules described by tables (jacks, knobs, help text); the patch graph (modules, cables, signal order, one block of delay round a loop); voices and the MIDI In module; Oscillator, Envelope, LFO, VCA, Mixer, Attenuverter, Output; patch changes handed to the audio thread without locks; live taps on every output; `konnekt-render` plays a patch to WAV; `konnekt_tst_engine` (pitch 1 per octave, voices, a per-voice cable into a one-voice input mixes, loops delay one block, unplugged inputs read their knobs). Result: as planned; `konnekt-render` plays a built-in patch (patch files arrive with N4); patches are built from plain data (`PatchSpec`, no Qt) for the renderer, the tests and the app; a stolen voice's gate closes for one block so envelopes start again; 8 voices of two oscillators, an LFO, a mixer, an envelope and a VCA: 3.4 % of a core; `konnekt_tst_engine` (16 tests, including every module's help text being there and every module fed by everything at once staying finite) |
| N2 ✅ | **The other modules** | Noise, the three filters, Sample & hold, Slew, Drive, Crusher, Ring mod, Clock, Sequencer, Chorus, Delay, Reverb, Scope; each tested. Result: as planned, all 23 modules; the ladder is Kalliope's and the OTA filter Kobalt's, as they are; the sequencer has 8 steps (a pitch and on / off each, length, gate length) rather than 16, to keep its panel playable; a crusher at n bits now gives exactly 2^n levels (Klang's too: rounding a full-scale wave had given one more); `konnekt_tst_modules` (15 tests: the filters cut and pass, sample & hold holds, slew glides, the clock ticks to the sample, the sequencer steps, rests and wraps, the delay echoes on time, the stereo effects make two sides) |
| N3 ✅ | **The app** | The `konnekt` app on JACK: the rack of modules drawn from their tables, add / move / delete, click-to-wire cables, live traces on the jacks, a scope on a cable, help on hover, VOICES, MIDI Learn for knobs. Result: as planned; panels are drawn from the module tables (two-position controls as buttons; the sequencer 8 columns wide: each step's note over its on / off); jacks coloured by what they carry (audio orange, pitch blue, gates green, modulation violet), outputs with a 1.5 s trace; cables sag between jacks; a plugged input lifts its cable when clicked; right-click an output for the scope (a Scope module shows its own); every module, knob and jack explains itself in the help bar; MIDI Learn per knob, saved with the patch; the patch in use kept in `current.json`; it connects to every MIDI controller; `konnekt_tst_konnekt` (5 tests: the first patch plays, cables replace, knob texts, the patch round-trips) |
| N4 ✅ | **Patches and lessons** | Save / load by name; lesson patches with their explanations; the manual (HELP); README and the suite docs. Result: as planned; PATCHES saves and loads by name (`~/.local/share/konnekt/patches/*.json`, names made safe for files); LESSONS: ten patches with their explanations (a tone; notes that start and stop; the filter; a filter envelope; LFOs; two oscillators; FM; a sequenced bass part; noise percussion; effects), each loaded with its text in a panel, ◂ ▸ through them; HELP opens the manual; `konnekt_tst_konnekt` loads every lesson whole (no unknown module, jack or knob) and plays it (8 tests) |
| N5 ✅ | **Explanations** | Right-click any knob, button, jack, module, top-bar button or module in the list for a full explanation: what it is, how it works, why you need it, where it goes, what to try, with what it's doing on the rack now and links to related parts. Result: as planned; the texts are `explain/*.json` (signals, Konnekt's controls, every module and every one of its knobs and jacks; numbered parts share one text, the OTA and state-variable filters take the Ladder's for their shared knobs and jacks); `Explainer` builds each card with live facts (value, range, default, the input a knob works with, MIDI link, what's plugged in, where an output goes); a panel on the right follows the rack live, with links and ◂ back; right-click on an output also shows it on the scope; `konnekt_tst_konnekt` checks every module, knob and jack has its texts, no text names a part that doesn't exist, every link and every card opens, and the facts match the rack (10 tests) |
| N6 ✅ | **Presets** | Finished sounds to play and take apart, in the styles of industrial, EBM, acid and big beat, each with how it's built and what to tweak. Result: 17 presets in five categories (Bass: Body Music Bass, Acid Squelch, Industrial Grind Bass, Big Beat Wobble, Sub Drop; Leads and stabs: Rave Stab, Siren Lead, Sync Scream, Whirling Rave Lead, Metal Clang; Pads and atmospheres: Industrial Drone, Breathy Acid Pad; Drums and grooves: Industrial Drum Loop, Body Music Groove; Effects: Computer Burble, Laser Zap, Noise Riser) in `presets/*.json`; PRESETS lists them by category and they share the lessons' text panel (◂ ▸); every preset's VOLUME is set so its loudest 100 ms is about -16 dB (four quiet ones at full volume land within 2 dB); the clocked ones run on one voice (a one-voice signal reaches every voice, so more would stack); the sequenced basses (and lesson 8) sit an octave higher so a key around C3 plays them in the bass register, and lesson 8 gained its missing MIDI In → TRANSPOSE cable; `konnekt_tst_konnekt` loads every preset whole, plays it (sound, no clipping) and checks the ones without a clock go quiet (11 tests) |
| N7 ✅ | **80s and synth-pop leads** | More leads: 80s synth-pop, new wave and cold wave. Result: 14 presets in a new category (Pop Pluck Riff, Pop Pluck Lead, Breathy Pipe Lead, Anvil Lead, Dark Detuned Lead, Square Glide Lead, Cold Wave Lead, Polysynth Brass, Anthem Lead, Glass Bell Lead, Fifths Lead, Talking Lead, Blip Lead, Minor Arp), 31 presets in all; levels set the same way (the two plucks were 6 to 12 dB short through a low filter, and gained a more open filter, higher mixer levels and a touch of Drive); `konnekt_tst_konnekt` plays all 31 |
| N8 ✅ | **More bass, leads and stabs** | Bass and Leads and stabs up to 14 each, as many as the 80s leads. Result: Bass gained Rubber Pluck Bass, Sync Bass, FM Slap Bass, Rolling Detuned Bass, Played Acid Bass, Octave Pulse Bass, Crushed Bass, Folded Bass and Growl Bass; Leads and stabs gained Industrial Power Stab, House Organ Stab, Acid Lead, Screaming Resonance Lead, Trance Saw Lead, Crushed Lead, Trance Gate Stab (a Clock into the envelope's RETRIG while chords are held), Metallic FM Stab and Random Step Lead (Sample & Hold into FM); 49 presets in all, levels set as before; `konnekt_tst_konnekt` plays all 49 |
| N9 ✅ | **Pads, drums and effects** | Pads and atmospheres, Drums and grooves and Effects up to 14 each: 84 presets, 14 in every category. Result: 12 pads (Warm Analog Strings, Glass Pad, Choir Pad, Sweeping PWM Pad, Dark Ambient Pad, Shimmer Pad, Ring Mod Atmosphere, Hollow Square Pad, Factory Hum, Tremolo Pad, Drifting Pad, Reverse Swell), 12 grooves (House Groove, Breakbeat, EBM Stomp, Acid Groove, Electro Beat, Industrial Metal Loop, Tribal Toms, Minimal Techno, Synth-Pop Beat, Trance Pulse, Glitch Loop, Big Beat Groove; built from shared drum voices: kick, snare, hat, clap, tom, cowbell, rim, a ring-mod clank), 11 effects (Falling Bomb, Air Raid Siren, Sci-Fi Warble, Robot Voice, Wind Gusts, Heartbeat, Metal Scrape, Power Up, Data Glitch, Power Down, Sonar Ping); Reverse Swell keeps its VCA before the (once-per-patch) reverb, so chords don't multiply it; Choir Pad, Wind Gusts and Metal Scrape gained gain inside (a narrow band-pass is quiet); the PRESETS list is six columns wide and scrolls in a smaller window; `konnekt_tst_konnekt` plays all 84 |

Fixed after N9: loading a preset froze the window for about 2 s, because the preset list (84 buttons) was rebuilt on every load; the lesson and preset lists are now read once. Clearing a rack rebuilt the screen once per module; now once. Each knob's 32 ribs are one shape instead of 32 items (every instrument's knobs), a module's on/off buttons no longer make a hidden knob, and traces only redraw for modules in view. Loading Electro Beat: 1.9 s → 41 ms; its running CPU (offscreen): 14 % → 7 %.
